## Full description

Turn any Android TV or Google TV into a live-view screen — without leaving what you are watching.

GTV Live Stream listens on your local network and shows a small floating video window over the
current app when your automation asks for it. Doorbell camera, driveway cam, baby monitor, a
scoreboard feed: if it speaks RTSP, HLS or MP4, it can pop up in a corner of the screen and vanish
again on its own.

WHAT IT IS

• A tiny HTTP receiver — POST a stream URL, get a live window
• A floating overlay that never steals the remote: D-pad input keeps going to the app underneath
• Auto-dismiss after a configurable delay, or close it with /hide
• RTSP, HLS (m3u8) and progressive MP4 playback
• Adjustable corner, size, title, subtitle and mute per request

WHAT IT IS NOT

• No account, no cloud, no subscription
• No analytics, no ads, no data collection
• No vendor lock-in: works with Home Assistant, Node-RED, curl, or your own scripts

REQUIRES

• Android TV / Google TV device (Android 6.0+)
• "Display over other apps" permission for the overlay
• A stream URL reachable from the TV on your local network

NOTE

Overlays cannot be drawn over DRM-protected apps such as Netflix, Prime Video or Disney+, and
never appear over HDMI inputs. This is an Android platform restriction, not a limitation of the
app.
